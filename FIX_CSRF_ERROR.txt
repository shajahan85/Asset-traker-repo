🎯 DEPLOYMENT CONCEPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Deployment is NOT:
❌ "Copy all files to server"
❌ "Delete and start fresh"
❌ "Test which files are needed"

Deployment IS:
✅ "Update only the files that changed"
✅ "Overwrite changed files, keep others intact"
✅ "Run management commands (collectstatic, migrate)"
✅ "Restart services"

Think of it like updating your phone app:
- The update doesn't reinstall everything
- It only updates the parts that changed
- The rest of the app stays intact
